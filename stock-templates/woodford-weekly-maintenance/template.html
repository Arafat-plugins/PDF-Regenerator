Understanding these metrics
FCP: How quickly the first visible content appears.
LCP: How quickly the main content becomes visible.
CLS: Measures unexpected page layout movement.
TBT: Measures how long the browser is blocked from responding during loading.
TTI: An older metric measuring when the page becomes fully interactive.
